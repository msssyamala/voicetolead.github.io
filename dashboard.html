Pacing

--

Steady speed, useful pauses, and endings that do not feel rushed.

Conciseness

--

Clear answers that avoid rambling, repetition, and extra filler ideas.

Eye Contact

--

Camera/audience connection, fewer downward glances, and engaged presence.

Demeanor

--

Confidence, calmness, facial expression, energy, and professional presence.

Tone

--

Whether your voice sounds confident, friendly, persuasive, and appropriate for the drill.
